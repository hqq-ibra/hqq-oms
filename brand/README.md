## What helps most

**Photos:** straight from the phone or camera. Don't send screenshots of photos, and don't
send them through WhatsApp if you can avoid it — WhatsApp crushes image quality badly, and
it shows on a large screen. Email them to yourself or use a USB copy instead.

**Logo:** this is the one file where the format really matters. A vector `.svg` stays razor
sharp at any size — on a phone, on a 4K monitor, on the browser tab icon. The version I
pulled out of your PDF is a 1315×715 bitmap; usable, but it will look soft when enlarged.

**Certificates:** worth including even if you think they're boring. On a supplier site,
visible credentials convert visitors into callers.
